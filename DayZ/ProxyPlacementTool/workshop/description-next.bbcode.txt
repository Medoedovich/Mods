An offline authoring tool for positioning items relative to buildings and wrecks. Use native Gizmo controls or precise values, inspect items in character mode and export CE dispatch XML.

[b]Early alpha. DayZDiag only.[/b]
[b]Projects cannot be saved or reopened yet. Export XML before exiting. Each launch starts an empty scene; exported XML files remain available.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Create buildings, wrecks and items by dragging catalog entries into the world editing area.
[*]Browse Objects by map/category, with residential and wreck subcategories; StaticObj hidden by default.
[*]Browse Items by category/subcategory, including weapons, ammo, clothing, tools and vehicle parts.
[*]Search items by localized name or classname; search objects by classname. Matching folders are revealed without a 100-result limit, and expanded branches are remembered.
[*]Native 3D item previews on hover, with the full classname.
[*]Native translation/rotation handles and precise coordinates/angles.
[*]Scene Objects list for selecting the character, anchor and individual item instances.
[*]One building/wreck anchor per scene, with Replace / Cancel confirmation when changing it.
[*]Free camera with an adjustable speed multiplier.
[*]Character mode for take-action and nearby-inventory checks; HUD hidden while editing and test character equipped with clothes and boots.
[*]Separate multi-item XML exports for each anchor.
[/list]

[h2]Start[/h2]
Requires DayZ, DayZ Tools with a matching DayZDiag executable and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3826868718]Test Range[/url].

[olist]
[*]Subscribe to this tool and Test Range, and wait for both downloads.
[*]Close any running DayZ client and keep Steam running.
[*]Open this tool's installed folder: steamapps/workshop/content/221100/3839146464.
[*]Run [b]Start-Editor.cmd[/b].
[/olist]

Enabling the mod in the regular Launcher alone does not open the editor.

See README.en.md or README.ru.md for setup. The editor follows the DayZDiag client language: Russian or English, with English for other languages. Tested with DayZDiag 1.30.164289.70.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]F1[/b] — switch between cursor and camera flight.
[*][b]WASD[/b] — move the camera; [b]Q/Z[/b] — move up/down during flight.
[*][b]Mouse[/b] — look around during flight.
[*][b]Mouse wheel[/b] — adjust the camera speed multiplier during flight.
[*][b]Shift/Ctrl[/b] — fly faster; [b]Alt[/b] — fly slower.
[*][b]F4[/b] — switch between the character and editor.
[*][b]F7[/b] — move the selected entity.
[*][b]F8[/b] — rotate the selected item.
[*][b]F9[/b] — reset the selection to its pose at selection time.
[*][b]F10[/b] — export the selected item and full anchor XML.
[*][b]F11[/b] — toggle diagnostics.
[/list]

[h2]Alpha limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Project saving and loading are not available yet. Export XML before exiting.
[*]No delete UI or Undo/Redo yet.
[*]Anchors use zero orientation and scale 1; anchor rotation is unsupported.
[*]The retail client, servers and multiplayer are unsupported. This tool requires DayZDiag.
[*]Exported XML is a fragment for the target mission's mapgroupproto.xml. Merge it with the appropriate economy settings, including types.xml, and test the result in CE.
[*]Editor preview placement is scripted; final loot placement is performed by the economy.
[/list]

Output: %LOCALAPPDATA%\ProxyPlacementTool\profiles\ProxyPlacement. Bug reports should include the game version, classnames, reproduction steps and relevant log lines.
