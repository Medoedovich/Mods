An offline DayZ editor for placing loot at any angle relative to buildings and wrecks, like vehicle parts on vanilla wrecks. Use the native Gizmo or enter precise coordinates and angles, check the placement as a character, and export CE dispatch XML.

[b]Early alpha. DayZDiag only.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Create buildings, wrecks and items by dragging catalog entries into the world editing area.
[*]Browse buildings and wrecks by map and category. Search items by localized name or classname and hover over a result for a 3D preview.
[*]Save and reopen JSON projects with the building or wreck, placed items, character position and camera view.
[*]Move and rotate loot with native Gizmo handles or numeric fields. Undo and redo transform edits.
[*]Show standard loot points and their spawn volumes to spot where new proxies might overlap existing loot. The editor uses the installed game's data when available.
[*]Use Place on surface to lay an item flat on the ground or floor directly below it.
[*]Switch to the test character to check the Take action and the Vicinity inventory panel.
[*]Export CE dispatch XML for the selected item or for all placed items associated with the building or wreck.
[/list]

Interface design inspired by the World Editor in Arma Reforger.

[h2]Start[/h2]
Requires DayZ, DayZ Tools with a matching DayZDiag executable and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3826868718]Test Range[/url].

[olist]
[*]Subscribe to this tool and Test Range, and wait for both downloads.
[*]Close any running DayZ client and keep Steam running.
[*]Open [b]steamapps/workshop/content/221100/3839146464[/b] in your Steam library.
[*]Run [b]Start-Editor.cmd[/b].
[/olist]

Enabling the mod in the regular Launcher alone does not open the editor.

The editor follows the DayZDiag client language and supports Russian and English. Other languages use English.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hold the right mouse button[/b] over the scene to fly. Release it to return to the cursor.
[*][b]WASD[/b] moves the camera, [b]Q[/b] rises, [b]Z[/b] descends, and the mouse looks around. The mouse wheel adjusts flight speed. The camera icon in [b]View[/b] opens the full control reference.
[*][b]F4[/b] — switch between the character and editor.
[*][b]F7 / F8[/b] — move the selected entity or rotate the selected item.
[*][b]F9[/b] — restore the selected entity's position and orientation from when it was selected.
[*][b]Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y[/b] — undo or redo a transform edit.
[*][b]F10[/b] — export the selected item and full anchor XML.
[*][b]F11[/b] — toggle diagnostics.
[/list]

[h2]Alpha limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]The editor runs offline in DayZDiag with Test Range.
[*]Each scene has one building or wreck at zero orientation and scale 1.
[*]Undo and Redo cover position and rotation changes.
[*]Merge the exported dispatch into the target mission's mapgroupproto.xml, configure the economy as needed, and test spawning in CE.
[/list]

Find XML exports and the Projects folder under [b]%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProxyPlacementTool\profiles\ProxyPlacement[/b]. For bug reports, include the game version, classnames, reproduction steps and relevant log lines.
