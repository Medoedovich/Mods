[h1]0.4.0-alpha — Projects and loot placement[/h1]

[list]
[*]Save and reopen projects with the building or wreck, placed items, test character position, and camera view.
[*]Undo and redo moves, rotations, and numeric edits with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.
[*]Show standard loot points and their spawn volumes using the installed game's data. Use the overlay to spot where new proxies might overlap existing loot.
[*]Lay the selected item flat on the ground or floor below with Place on surface.
[*]Hold the right mouse button inside the editing area to fly. The Camera controls button opens a quick reference in the editor.
[*]Use a redesigned icon toolbar, translucent panels, a larger Scene Objects list, and dedicated notifications. Catalog hover previews show 3D models with the new theme.
[/list]
