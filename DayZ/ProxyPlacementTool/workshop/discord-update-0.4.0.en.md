**Proxy Placement Tool — 0.4.0-alpha is live!**

Place loot at different angles relative to buildings and wrecks, like vehicle parts on vanilla wrecks, then export CE dispatch XML.

This update adds

- **Save and open projects** to continue working on a scene later.
- **Undo and Redo** for moves, rotations and numeric edits.
- **Standard loot points and spawn volumes** to help avoid overlap with your proxies. The editor reads data from the installed game when available.
- **Place on surface** to lay an item flat on the ground or floor below it.
- **Hold RMB to fly**, with a camera controls reference inside the editor.
- A new translucent interface, grouped toolbar icons, a larger scene list and dedicated notifications.

Still alpha, with an offline DayZDiag workflow. Feedback and placement examples are welcome in this thread!
