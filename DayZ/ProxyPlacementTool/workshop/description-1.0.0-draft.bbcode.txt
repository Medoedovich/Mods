An offline DayZ editor for placing loot at different angles relative to buildings and wrecks, then exporting CE dispatch XML.

Lean a rifle against a wall, place a backpack inside a wardrobe, or position a wheel diagonally on a car seat. Use the native Gizmo or enter precise coordinates and angles to create your own loot arrangements.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Create buildings, wrecks and items by dragging catalog entries into the world editing area.
[*]Browse objects by map and category. Find items under Common, ChernarusPlus, Livonia, Sakhal and Nasdara, with subgroups for weapon calibers, clothing families, equipment and more.
[*]Search items by their localized name or classname. Hover over entries for 3D previews of items and supported buildings and wrecks.
[*]Move and rotate items with the native Gizmo or precise numeric fields. Use Place on surface to lay an item flat on the ground or floor below it.
[*]Save and reopen JSON projects containing the building or wreck, placed items, character position and camera view.
[*]Delete selected items and use Undo/Redo for placement, rotation, numeric edits, item creation and deletion.
[*]Protect unsaved changes with Save / Don't save / Cancel before opening another project, replacing the building or wreck, or leaving through the in-game menu.
[*]Show standard loot points and their spawn volumes to help avoid overlapping existing loot. The editor reads the installed game's data when available.
[*]Switch to the test character to inspect placement, the Take action and the Vicinity inventory panel.
[*]Export CE dispatch XML for the selected item or the entire building/wreck layout.
[/list]

Interface design inspired by the World Editor in Arma Reforger.

[h2]Start[/h2]
Requires DayZ, DayZ Tools with a matching DayZDiag executable and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3826868718]Test Range[/url].

[olist]
[*]Subscribe to this tool and Test Range, and wait for both downloads.
[*]Close any running DayZ client and keep Steam running.
[*]Open [b]steamapps/workshop/content/221100/3839146464[/b] in your Steam library.
[*]Run [b]Start-Editor.cmd[/b].
[/olist]

Enabling the mod in the regular Launcher alone does not open the editor.

The interface supports English and Russian, following the DayZDiag client language. Other interface languages fall back to English.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
Hold the right mouse button over the scene to fly. Hover over toolbar buttons for shortcuts, or open [b]Camera controls[/b] in [b]View[/b] for navigation help.

[h2]Scope and limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]The editor runs offline in DayZDiag with Test Range.
[*]Each scene has one building or wreck at zero orientation and scale 1. Rotation is available for placed items.
[*]Standard loot volumes are a reference overlay; they do not create or edit standard loot points.
[*]Projects preserve placement data. Item inventory contents, attachments, damage and quantities are outside the saved project data.
[/list]

Merge the exported dispatch into the appropriate group in the target mission's [b]mapgroupproto.xml[/b], configure the economy as needed, including [b]types.xml[/b], and test actual spawning through CE.

Character mode checks the editor preview. After integrating the XML, verify the spawned items' positions, rotations, intersections, Take actions and Vicinity visibility in the target mission.

Find XML exports and the Projects folder under:
[b]%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProxyPlacementTool\profiles\ProxyPlacement[/b]
